The Smile Hub

One Place For All Your Dental Care In Kandivali West

The Smile Hub is a multi-speciality dental clinic in Kandivali West, Mumbai, where dental, orthodontic and oral & maxillofacial care come together. From everyday dental problems and preventive check-ups to braces, aligners, implants and surgical care, you are guided to the right specialist from the first visit.

Dr. Angad Shetye BDS, MDS — Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon
Dr. Pooja Sable Shetye BDS, MDS — Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics Orthodontist

Founded by our two specialists, The Smile Hub brings together complementary areas of expertise — so you can access the right dental, orthodontic or surgical care without looking elsewhere for every concern.

Whatever brings you to us, our focus is on understanding your concern, explaining your options and helping you feel comfortable with the care you choose.

What Happens When You Visit The Smile Hub?

STEP 01

Tell Us What's Bothering You

Whether it's pain, a missing tooth, crooked teeth or a concern you've been putting off, tell us what you're experiencing.

STEP 02

Get Assessed By The Right Doctor

Your concern will be assessed based on your symptoms and the type of dental care you may need.

STEP 03

Understand Your Options

We'll explain what we find and walk you through the treatment options that may be right for you.

STEP 04

Move Forward With A Clear Plan

Once you understand your options, you can choose the next step with greater confidence.

Modern Dental Technology For Precise Treatment

At The Smile Hub, we use advanced dental technology to understand your smile better, plan treatments more precisely and make your experience more comfortable.

Dental Laser

Helps us perform select soft-tissue procedures with precision and control, while reducing trauma to surrounding tissue.

Physiodispenser

Used during implant procedures to give our specialists greater control and precision throughout the process.

iTero Intraoral Scanner

A quick, comfortable scan that gives us a detailed 3D view of your teeth and bite.

Digital Impressions

No more messy impression trays. We use digital scans to get a clear, detailed picture of your teeth.

Digital Smile Design

See how your smile could look before you begin treatment and understand what’s possible for your smile.

Digital Treatment Planning

We use digital tools to carefully plan your treatment based on your teeth, bite and individual needs.
